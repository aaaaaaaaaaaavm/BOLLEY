# What would make Bolley pointless

Open problems are work. These are reasons to stop or change the architecture.

## 1. Rail force density

**Threshold:** a representative covered-gap corner channel must produce at least 255 N for
150 ms without exceeding its material, current or temperature limits.

The first-order area screen must remain at or below 26 kPa required air-gap shear in the 6 kg,
worst-CG case. The value is deliberately below the approximately 35 kPa external benchmark; the
difference is the provisional margin for cover, fringing, manufacturing and control.

**Status:** OPEN. No electromagnetic model or hardware result exists.

## 2. High-speed commutation

**Preferred band:** no more than 200 V DC link at the reference duty.

**Kill threshold:** more than 400 V required to establish and extinguish channel current at the
maximum commanded speed, or no positive force margin after voltage saturation.

**Status:** OPEN. Pole-passage frequency alone does not answer it.

## 3. Cooperative-interface mass

**Target:** no more than 0.25 kg incremental payload mass.

**Kill threshold:** more than 0.40 kg after structural retention and shielding are included.

Above the threshold, the interface begins surrendering too much of its advantage over compact
propulsion and the unmodified-payload fallback.

**Status:** OPEN. Phase 0 is a material-volume screen.

## 4. Electrical shot energy

**Kill threshold:** more than 900 J gross stored-energy draw for the 4 kg reference shot.

The threshold prevents a lower-force topology from quietly retaining VOLLEY's multi-string bank
problem through poor efficiency.

**Status:** OPEN. The first-order result uses an assumed 40–60% total efficiency.

## 5. Tip-off

**Kill threshold:** more than 2 deg/s per axis at free flight.

Force-centroid control is only a proposed remedy. Exit fringing, channel calibration, guide
clearance and CG error must all fit inside the same number.

**Status:** OPEN.

## 6. Magnetic compatibility

**Static threshold:** more than 50 microtesla above ambient outside the unpowered payload envelope.

**Transient threshold:** supplied by the manifested spacecraft; no universal induced-voltage
number is invented here.

**Status:** OPEN.

## 7. Interface acceptance

**Kill threshold:** no plausible path to a launch-provider-approved aluminium contact surface and
mechanically retained internal magnetic circuit.

A machine that works only by violating its payload interface has not worked.

**Status:** OPEN.

## 8. Commercial reason to exist

**Kill threshold:** quoted recurring hardware plus integration is not lower than the customer's
credible alternative for same-plane phasing.

No assumed rupee or dollar comparison can close this item.

**Status:** NOT EVALUABLE until quotations and a customer case exist.
