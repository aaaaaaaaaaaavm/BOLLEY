## The selected machine

Each of the four face channels contains 27 stationary cells in a repeating three-phase lattice.
The 45.3 mm pitch makes a 1.2231 m installed primary. A 318.6 mm five-lane passive cage starts
2.25 mm inside the payload end and finishes the 900 mm powered travel with another 2.25 mm of
engagement. The cage can intersect at most 9 cells at once,
so the sectional drive energises no more than
3 cells per phase in each channel.

That distinction closes A8a without pretending the unused primary disappears. All 27 cells count
against the installed-mass band, while only the active window contributes pulse copper/core loss
and temperature. At the selected 10.4 mm2 conductor area, active-window resistance falls to
0.7873 of A7b and the worst hot reference corner falls to
895.47 J.

## Why this point wins

The selection does not minimise one attractive number. It minimises the weakest continuous hard
band, then installed primary mass, then hot reference energy. Its five tightest demands are:

| Band | Normalized demand | Remaining model margin |
|---|---:|---:|
| Reference Source Energy | 0.9950 | 0.50% |
| Installed Active Primary Mass | 0.9943 | 0.57% |
| Predicted Stationary Core Peak | 0.9896 | 1.04% |
| Secondary Efficiency | 0.9822 | 1.78% |
| Predicted Mean Field Low | 0.9568 | 4.32% |

This is a narrow, coupled solution rather than an oversized optimum. More pitch or copper breaks
the 16 kg primary limit; less copper or cage length raises resistance and source energy; more
current consumes the already narrow nonlinear-field surrogate margin. The feasible island contains
only 27-cell candidates, 44.8–45.6 mm pitch, 375–383 A and 10.2–10.8 mm2 conductors.

## What remains open

- A6h must solve the selected 380 A point on fresh coarse/fine/alternate nonlinear meshes. The
  1.534 T stationary-core value is only a current-scaled A6g estimate.
- A7c must rerun the selected point from A6h field and inductance outputs rather than A8b surrogates.
- Cell handoff, end effects, force ripple, inverter partitioning and fault containment remain
  outside the model.
- Structure, winding end turns, cooling, fasteners and power electronics are absent from the
  15.91 kg active-primary accounting.
- Gen3 CAD may encode this point for fit inspection, but cannot convert these model margins into
  hardware evidence.
