# ADR-026 — why I promoted Quintweb beyond the transverse field gate

- **Date:** 2026-08-13
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decider:** Adityavardhan Mishra
- **Evidence:** A6g worst-of-three-mesh nonlinear field result

## What I decided

I accepted A6g's 13/13 result and promoted Gen2.6 Quintweb to five-lane cage/circuit reclosure and
Gen3 CAD. I preserved the 0.25/0.30 kg mass failures and 0.40 kg absolute limit.

## Why I chose it

All formal meshes retain the field floor. Worst moving material is 1.2888 T, stationary core is
1.5138 T, mean field is 0.7426–0.7454 T and fine per-cell inductance is 1.3062 times A3g inside
the predeclared four-turn band.

## What follows

- A7b must prove that 25% more passive area closes A7a's four failures.
- CAD must resolve twenty layered blades and can reject the 11.77 g remaining mass margin.
- The transverse 2D pass does not resolve axial cage pattern, ends or force.
