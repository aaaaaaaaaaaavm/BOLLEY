# ADR-015 — freeze Gen2.2 Fluxmanifold-R4

- **Date:** 2026-08-13
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decider:** Adityavardhan Mishra
- **Evidence:** Failed A6b result and analytical change accounting

## Decision

Freeze Gen2.2 at 1,200 A-turn RMS and a 4 mm return haunch. Preserve every other Gen2.1 dimension,
the passive cage and the copper-neutral three-turn winding. Require physical upper/lower bands to
pass on the worst of base, fine and expanded meshes in A6c.

## Consequences

- Mean field is intentionally traded for ligament and haunch margin, but the 0.72 T floor remains.
- Active stationary primary rises by only 0.041 kg from Gen2.1.
- A6c can still reject the point; no circuit or CAD work is promoted before its result.
