# ADR-045: I promote Fluxframe to bus selection with zero credit

- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Status:** accepted

## Context

A12 passed five sourced mass-envelope bands. The 121.36 g Gen5 target is 30.80–42.58% of the
published 3U structure masses I froze. Three equivalence/acceptance bands remain open.

## Decision

I select no universal mass saving. I promote Gen5 only to choosing one bus and producing a
before/after parts ledger plus one multifunctional load-path cross-section. I retain zero displaced
mass in the controlled baseline.

## Consequences

- The public envelope says sufficient gross chassis mass may exist to make the work rational.
- A full-replacement arithmetic row is not proof that Fluxframe can replace a chassis.
- The EnduroSat less-than value remains an upper bound.
- CAD and net-mass claims remain blocked by structure, thermal, electrical, magnetic, rail and
  provider evidence.
