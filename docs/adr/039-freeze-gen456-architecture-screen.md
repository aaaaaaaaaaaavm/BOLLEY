# ADR-039: I freeze the Gen4-Gen6 premise screen

- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Status:** accepted as a pre-run declaration

## Context

sibling repository then supplied a stronger system result: deleting the mover attacks 11% of its
dry mass, while the pulse obligation carries 28.1%. Continuing only with sectional winding detail
would close Gen4 but would not answer whether Bolley's premise is still worth its spacecraft mass.

I retain Gen4 as the honest closure path for Fluxrelay. I define Gen5 Fluxframe as a net-mass
architecture and Gen6 Fluxpiston as a bulk-energy architecture. I freeze A10 before writing its
calculator and require it to expose the 12 m/s / 0.90 m / 8 g consistency check first.

## Consequences

- I do not allow ideal pressure work to become seal evidence.

  beat a mechanism.

  optimisations.
