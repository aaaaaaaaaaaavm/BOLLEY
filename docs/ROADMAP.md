# My development roadmap

## Phase 0 — decide whether the coupon deserves to exist

- I freeze the cooperative interface and duty.
- I run first-order mass, force-area and CG allocation screens.
- I reject incomplete flux paths with A3a and fit-check the surviving moving interface.
- I build nonlinear 2D and 3D electromagnetic models.
- I size commutation voltage, phase current and DC link from flux-linkage maps.

**Exit:** I have bounded every critical input to a one-channel coupon. I send the low-profile
comb-fin to nonlinear FEA only after I have a legal two-sided interface cross-section; otherwise I
promote the induction lane. I do no CAD beauty work before this.

## Phase 1 — one corner

- I manufacture one comb-fin face coupon and one stator tile.
- I measure static and dynamic force across gap and tolerance.
- I measure current rise, force ripple, normal force, rail loss and remanence.
- I regenerate the model from measurements.

**Exit:** at least 255 N for 150 ms, within the predeclared voltage, temperature and magnetic
bands. Otherwise I change topology or stop.

## Phase 2 — four channels and a dummy payload

- I build instrumented 4 kg and 6 kg dummies with adjustable CG.
- I run 0–12 m/s shots over 0.90 m.
- I inject channel faults, shape the field end and measure velocity independently.
- I measure tip-off in six degrees of freedom.

**Exit:** I demonstrate reference performance, less than 2 deg/s/axis, no guide loss and no
post-release mover.

## Phase 3 — environment and repeatability

- I test ascent vibration of the gate, comb-fin roots and contact rails.
- I operate the pulse system in thermal vacuum.
- I run a 100-cycle wear and magnetic-drift campaign.
- I test payload EMC with representative avionics.

## Phase 4 — mission integration

- I analyse host recoil and attitude restoration.
- I obtain a supplier-quoted recurring BOM.
- I obtain launch-provider interface disposition.
- I fly a demonstration with an inert or technology payload.
