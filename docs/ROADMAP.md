# Development roadmap

## Phase 0 — decide whether the coupon deserves to exist

- Freeze the cooperative interface and duty.
- Run first-order mass, force-area and CG allocation screens.
- Reject incomplete flux paths with A3a and fit-check the surviving moving interface.
- Build nonlinear 2D and 3D electromagnetic models.
- Size commutation voltage, phase current and DC link from flux-linkage maps.

**Exit:** every critical input to a one-channel coupon is bounded. The opposed fin/tab receives
nonlinear FEA only after a legal two-sided interface cross-section exists; otherwise the induction
lane is promoted. No CAD beauty work before this.

## Phase 1 — one corner

- Manufacture one hybrid rail coupon and one stator tile.
- Measure static and dynamic force across gap and tolerance.
- Measure current rise, force ripple, normal force, rail loss and remanence.
- Regenerate the model from measurements.

**Exit:** at least 255 N for 150 ms, within the predeclared voltage, temperature and magnetic
bands. Otherwise change topology or stop.

## Phase 2 — four channels and a dummy payload

- Instrumented 4 kg and 6 kg dummies with adjustable CG.
- 0–12 m/s shots over 0.90 m.
- Channel fault injection, field-end shaping and independent velocity measurement.
- Six-degree-of-freedom tip-off measurement.

**Exit:** reference performance, less than 2 deg/s/axis, no guide loss and no post-release mover.

## Phase 3 — environment and repeatability

- Ascent vibration of the gate and hybrid rails.
- Thermal-vacuum pulse operation.
- 100-cycle wear and magnetic drift campaign.
- Payload EMC test with representative avionics.

## Phase 4 — mission integration

- Host recoil and attitude-restoration analysis.
- Supplier-quoted recurring BOM.
- Launch-provider interface disposition.
- Flight demonstration with an inert or technology payload.
