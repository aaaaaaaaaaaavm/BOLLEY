# Licensing

I license this entire repository under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
License (CC BY 4.0). The legal text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), and the requested attribution is
in [`NOTICE`](NOTICE).

I use one root licence because Bolley's Python, CAD generators, controlled parameters, generated
results and engineering prose are one disclosure. The executable model is the design expressed in
code; splitting it from the drawings and conclusions would create a false boundary.

| Scope | Licence |
|---|---|
| `analysis/` and generated results | CC BY 4.0 |
| `cad/`, models, manifests and renders | CC BY 4.0 |
| `docs/` and `validation/` | CC BY 4.0 |
| `tools/` and `.github/` | CC BY 4.0 |
| Root documents and metadata | CC BY 4.0 |

## No invented superseded licence

Bolley had no root licence before this decision. I therefore do not add VOLLEY's
`LICENSE-MIT-superseded`: Bolley never granted those MIT rights. Earlier public snapshots carried
no explicit permission beyond rights supplied by law. The current repository is distributed under
CC BY 4.0 from this licensing commit onward.

## Patent and engineering scope

CC BY 4.0 section 2(b)(2) does not license patent or trademark rights. That is deliberate. The
licence also does not imply that I have built, measured, qualified or flown Bolley, or that a
launch provider, CubeSat vendor, university, employer or standards body endorses it.

## Attribution

Redistribution or adaptation must credit me, link CC BY 4.0 and identify changes. For an
engineering record, marking changes matters: a modified model must not be presented as my frozen
result. Use [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff) for citation metadata.
